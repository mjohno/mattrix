# Troubleshooting checklist

- [ ] Record the problem report, user effect, scope, and start time.
- [ ] Triage severity and stabilize the service first.
- [ ] Examine evidence before selecting a cause.
- [ ] Reduce the problem to the smallest failing case.
- [ ] Ask what changed, where it fails, and why it fails.
- [ ] Form a specific diagnosis that evidence can disprove.
- [ ] Test the diagnosis and apply the least risky treatment.
- [ ] Record negative results and the final cure for future use.
