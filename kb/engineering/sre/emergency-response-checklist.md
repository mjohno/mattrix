# Emergency response checklist

- [ ] Recognize the emergency and assess user and service effect.
- [ ] Stop unsafe tests or changes when they are the likely cause.
- [ ] Stabilize the service before deep diagnosis.
- [ ] Escalate early when the response needs more authority or expertise.
- [ ] Keep responders informed with current facts and actions.
- [ ] Preserve evidence for later analysis.
- [ ] Record what worked, what failed, and what the team learned.
- [ ] Add prevention actions, including tests and failure scenarios, to follow-up work.
