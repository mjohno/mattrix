# On-call checklist

- [ ] Define primary and secondary responder responsibilities.
- [ ] Keep the rotation large enough to limit interrupt load.
- [ ] Measure page volume, after-hours work, and response quality.
- [ ] Make pages actionable and remove predictable operational work.
- [ ] Provide runbooks, access, training, and escalation paths.
- [ ] Give responders authority to mitigate service risk.
- [ ] Compensate on-call work fairly under the local policy.
- [ ] Review both operational overload and lack of practical service knowledge.
