# Decision Checklist

Use this checklist to check one `decide` result.

- [ ] The decision question and material context are explicit.
- [ ] The recommendation selects one best action or option.
- [ ] The reason considers applicable value, risk, dependency, reversibility, and feasible scope.
- [ ] Material alternatives are named with concise reasons for not selecting them.
- [ ] Assumptions, missing criteria, and uncertainty are visible.
- [ ] The result does not execute work, grant approval, investigate, or claim unsupported certainty.
