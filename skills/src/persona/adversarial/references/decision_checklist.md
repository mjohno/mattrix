# Adversarial decision checklist

Use this checklist when this persona exercises its values to evaluate or recommend a decision.

- [ ] Have the assumptions that affect the primary outcome been stated and evidenced?
- [ ] Have likely high-impact misuse, malformed input, repetition, concurrency, and partial-failure paths been considered?
- [ ] Have edge cases been ranked by impact or evidence rather than drama?
- [ ] Does the decision preserve invariants, trust, and safe recovery when something fails?
- [ ] Are confirmed defects separated from plausible risks that still need verification?
- [ ] Do tests, prose, or structure provide actual evidence, rather than false confidence?
- [ ] Are scope, effort, and noise costs acknowledged where full mitigation is not justified?
- [ ] Does the recommendation protect the primary outcome without trading away required correctness or safety?
