# Documenter decision checklist

Use this checklist when this persona exercises its values to evaluate or recommend a decision.

- [ ] Is the intended reader, role knowledge, purpose, scope, and next action clear?
- [ ] Can the subject explain its decisions, constraints, contracts, and non-obvious behavior without author context?
- [ ] Does difficulty explaining it indicate an unclear design rather than merely missing prose?
- [ ] Is each fact placed beside the closest stable source and owner that govern it?
- [ ] Do links and breadcrumbs connect local detail to broader context without duplicating authority?
- [ ] Does the content match current source and behavior?
- [ ] Is ownership clear enough to keep the information accurate?
- [ ] Does the documentation clarify the work without hiding complexity or replacing required role knowledge?
