# Executive Slides Checklist

Use this for executive-decision and executive-status conformance checks. A deck passes when every applicable critical item passes.

## Shared Critical

- [ ] The deck has one `#` title and uses `---` between slides.
- [ ] Every non-title slide has an `##` takeaway heading.
- [ ] Every bullet has six or fewer key words, and no slide has more than six bullets.
- [ ] Bullets are not nested.
- [ ] Supporting context has a direct Markdown link to its full cloud document on the relevant slide.
- [ ] Embedded excerpts are brief and necessary to understand the slide takeaway.

## Executive-Decision Critical

- [ ] The requested decision is explicit.
- [ ] Context, essential evidence or trade-offs, recommendation, and next owner/action support that decision.
- [ ] Every slide advances the decision pathway or supplies necessary linked evidence.

## Executive-Status Critical

- [ ] The update has exactly one content slide.
- [ ] The slide states overall status, progress highlights, material risk/blocker or decision needed, and next milestone or owner.
- [ ] The update has no appendix or separate references slide.

## Project-Roadmap-Update Critical

- [ ] The update states overall roadmap health and strategic outcome.
- [ ] Completed or changed milestones, upcoming milestones, and material dependencies are clear.
- [ ] Material risks, trade-offs, or escalations are visible.
- [ ] The update ends with a decision needed or explicit confirmation that none is needed.
- [ ] Supporting roadmaps, plans, and metrics have direct cloud links when they exist.

## Visionary-Story Critical

- [ ] The deck establishes current reality, strategic tension, envisioned future, credible path, and commitment requested.
- [ ] The future-state thesis appears early and is clear.
- [ ] Evidence supports the narrative and has direct cloud links when supporting material exists.
- [ ] The final slide requests one explicit commitment, decision, or next step.

## Technical-Review Critical

- [ ] The technical decision or review objective is explicit.
- [ ] Architecture, schema, code, or diagram excerpts provide necessary implementation context.
- [ ] Dependencies and implementation pathways are clear.
- [ ] Technical trade-offs, edge cases, and known risks are explicit.
- [ ] Technical excerpts are labeled and link to full cloud sources when they exist.

## Educational Critical

- [ ] Each slide has one clear learning objective or supports the active objective.
- [ ] Foundational concepts precede complex application.
- [ ] The deck includes a concrete example, scenario, or guided application.
- [ ] The deck ends with a key takeaway and behavioral action.

## Layered-Briefing Critical

- [ ] Every slide begins with a top-level takeaway heading.
- [ ] Supporting context is organized beneath the takeaway for scan-first reading.
- [ ] The deck covers strategic direction, alignment milestones, or shared outcomes.
- [ ] Implementation detail is excluded unless it changes alignment or direction.
- [ ] The deck ends with a shared next step or explicit confirmation.

## Quality

- [ ] Phrases are concise and parallel where practical.
- [ ] Headings state a conclusion, status, decision, or ask.
- [ ] Links use descriptive labels.
